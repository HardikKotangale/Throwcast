# Throwcast browser extension

Shows the page you're on on your TV, in Chrome and Brave.

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (Chrome) or `brave://extensions` (Brave).
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and pick this `extension` folder.
4. Pin Throwcast to your toolbar.

## Use it

Keep the Throwcast app open with your TV selected, then click the extension.

- **Show This Tab on TV** works on almost any site. The tab moves to a private TV-sized display that only the TV shows, so it keeps playing while you use other apps and desktops. The video fills the picture, with **Show All**, **Fill** or **Stretch**. Playback and picture controls appear in both the popup and the Throwcast app, and **Stop Casting** in either brings the tab back. Closing the tab also ends the cast.
- **Send Video Link Instead** appears when the page has a usable link. The TV then plays it by itself and the Mac does far less, if the site allows it.

DRM-protected video usually shows black.
